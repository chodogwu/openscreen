# OFT – Online Finance Tutor: 30s Promo (vertical 9:16)

**Goal:** get beginners and students to book a first session.
**Tone:** friendly, clear, confident. No hype, no get-rich promises.
**CTA:** "Book your first lesson."

## Script (about 75 words, 30s)

| # | Time | Visual | Voiceover / on-screen text |
|---|------|--------|----------------------------|
| 1 | 0-3s | Close-up: a student frowning at a bank statement and a calculator | VO: "Finance class lost you at compound interest?" Text: "Lost in finance class?" |
| 2 | 3-8s | Animated chart: a small savings line curving upward over 10 years | VO: "Here's the thing. Money maths is simple once someone explains it properly." |
| 3 | 8-14s | Tutor on a video call with a student, a whiteboard showing a budget, a loan and an investment sketch | VO: "OFT is a one-to-one online finance tutor. Budgeting, investing, loans, exam prep." Text: "1-to-1 online tutoring" |
| 4 | 14-21s | Quick cuts: student smiling at a laptop, a checked-off study plan, a quiz score going up | VO: "Lessons built around your level, your pace, your goals." Text: "Your level. Your pace." |
| 5 | 21-26s | Student confidently explaining a chart to a friend | VO: "Understand it once. Use it for life." |
| 6 | 26-30s | OFT logo on a clean background | VO: "OFT. Book your first lesson." Text: "Book your first lesson" + URL |

## Voiceover
Warm, calm, conversational. About 150 words per minute, so 75 words fits 30 seconds.

## Open questions
- Is "OFT" the real brand name? What does it stand for?
- Logo, colours and URL
- Who is the audience: school or university students, or adults managing their own money?
- Do you want a real tutor on camera or AI-generated people?
- Any compliance wording needed, such as "educational only, not financial advice"?
