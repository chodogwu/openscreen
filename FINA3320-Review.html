01 · Time value of money

A dollar today beats a dollar later

Future value

FV = PV × (1 + r)^n

Grow today’s cash forward at rate r for n periods.

Present value

PV = FV / (1 + r)^n

Discount a future cash flow back to today.

Annuity

PV = PMT × [1 − (1 + r)^−n] / r

Level payments at the end of each period.

Exam habit

Match the rate to the payment period. Monthly payments need a monthly rate and a monthly n.

02 · Bonds

Price is the PV of coupons plus face value

Worked example

5-year bond, 6% annual coupon, $1,000 face value, 8% yield to maturity.

P = 60 × [1 − 1.08^−5] / 0.08 + 1,000 / 1.08^5

P ≈ $239.56 + $680.58 = $920.15

02 · Stocks

Growing dividends, constant growth

P0 = D1 / (r − g)

D1 = D0 × (1 + g). The model needs g below r, and it prices the stock one period before D1 arrives.

Example: D1 = $2.00, r = 10%, g = 4%.

03 · Risk and return

CAPM prices only market risk

Security market line

E(R) = rf + β × (E(Rm) − rf)

Example: rf = 4%, β = 1.2, market risk premium = 6%.

E(R) = 4% + 1.2 × 6% = 11.2%

Systematic risk

Market-wide, so it cannot be diversified away.

Portfolio beta

Weighted average of the stocks’ betas.

05 · Cost of capital

WACC blends debt and equity

Formula

WACC = (E/V) × Re + (D/V) × Rd × (1 − Tc)

Use market values for the weights. Debt is cheaper after the interest tax shield.

Example

60% equity at 12%, 40% debt at 6% pre-tax, tax rate 25%.

0.60 × 12% + 0.40 × 6% × 0.75

= 7.2% + 1.8% = 9.0%
